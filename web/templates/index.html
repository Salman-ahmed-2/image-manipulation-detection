03 · Pipeline

How classification works

Every upload goes through deterministic preprocessing, provenance inspection, and single-pass scoring.

01

Model-specific preprocessing

The CIFAKE Custom CNN resizes inputs to 32×32 RGB with bilinear filtering and [0, 1] scaling. The Hugging Face ViT uses its own 224×224 image processor.

02

Uncalibrated model score

The selected classifier reports a raw sigmoid or softmax score for the AI-generated class. Treat it as an indicative score, never guaranteed certainty.

03

Downloadable PDF summary
