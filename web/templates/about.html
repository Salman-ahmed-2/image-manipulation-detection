Project background

Real-Photo vs. AI-Generated Classification

An educational web application offering two classifiers for real-photo vs. AI-generated image classification.

Dataset & Model

Available classifiers

CIFAKE Custom CNN: trained on 32×32 RGB real photographs from CIFAR-10 and AI-generated images from CIFAKE.

Hugging Face ViT: a separately downloaded third-party Vision Transformer classifier. It uses its own 224×224 image processor and was trained on a different real-vs-AI dataset.

Preprocessing and model score

The selected model applies its own preprocessing. Its score represents the model's output for AI-generated content; it is not calibrated or a guarantee of authenticity.

Dataset-Specific Benchmark & Limits

On CIFAKE's 20,000-image held-out test set, the trained Custom CNN recorded 95.3% accuracy.

That result is specific to CIFAKE's 32×32 benchmark and does not represent expected accuracy on arbitrary real-world photographs or images from other generative models.
